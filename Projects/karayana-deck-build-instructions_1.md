# Karayana Partner Deck: Build Instructions

Source: the approved content plan. Nothing here is built yet. Say "go" and the deck is built exactly as written below.

## 1. Purpose and audience

The audience is our partner, who is not technical. The deck must show five things:

1. What we are building.
2. How it works.
3. What is already built and what remains.
4. What the ₹85,000 covers.
5. What we need from the partner to begin.

## 2. Ground rules

- Short and visual. One idea per slide, a headline of at most six words, at most 20 other words, and one picture the partner can follow without reading.
- Wording says "our" and "we" so the partner feels we are one team.
- No GST anywhere. All prices are plain rupee amounts.
- No named hosting provider. The deck says "we choose the best setup together" after the week 1 audit.
- Do not reuse the notes Vaibhav gave as background. Write fresh plain wording.
- Before and after images are stamped **"Demo · illustrative"**.
- No per-day rate is shown or mentioned anywhere. Only task prices and totals appear.
- Never state a value we cannot back with days and rates. No named agency comparison. No claim that the prototype is finished software.

## 3. Deliverables

1. An editable, shareable online deck we can both work in.
2. A PowerPoint export.
3. A link to the live demo (prototype published). The QR code is optional.

## 8. The value case (slide 11)

The 50 working days ahead have a market value of ₹2,50,000. The partner pays ₹85,000, which is 66% less.

| Data and old-site migration | 11, 12 | 8 | 40,000 | 16,000 |
| Cloud, security and storage | 8, 9, 13 | 9 | 45,000 | 18,000 |
| Payouts, delivery and receipts | 6, 7, 10 | 7 | 35,000 | 14,000 |
| Start-up and management | 1, 2, 17 | 7 | 35,000 | 14,000 |
| Training, testing and launch | 14, 15, 16 | 7 | 35,000 | 14,000 |
| **Total** | | **50** | **2,50,000** | **1,00,000** |

How the slide makes it land, using true facts only:

- **A tall bar, then a short one.** The gap is seen before it is read.
- **A fresh start.** The prototype earns no credit in the value shown.
- **Each task shows what it replaces.** Real-life example: "Identity check" replaces a staff member squinting at a WhatsApp photo of an Aadhaar card, and "Real payments" replaces someone matching bank statements by hand.
- **Our own add-on prices prove the scale.** Real-life example: an add-on like GST invoices is already quoted at ₹15,000–20,000, which matches the market values above.

If the partner asks how the market value was set, the answer is: our blended market rate for the days involved.
